`<talos-compass>` renders a **direction**, not a number that happens to be an
angle. On a bar or a sweep gauge, 359° and 1° sit at opposite ends of the track;
on the compass they are neighbours, as they are in the world. The dial is fixed
(N up) and the needle rotates, so the cardinal labels stay upright at every
heading.

## Default

<Demo live>
  <talos-compass heading="72" label="heading"></talos-compass>
</Demo>

```html
<talos-compass heading="72" label="heading"></talos-compass>
```

The demo walks `heading` every second. The needle eases to each new bearing the
**short way round** (350° to 10° turns +20°, never −340°); the readout and colour
are exact at once. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the tween is skipped, not the
meaning.

## Target and tolerance bands

<Demo>
  <talos-compass heading="88" target="90" warn="10" crit="25" label="on target"></talos-compass>
  <talos-compass heading="105" target="90" warn="10" crit="25" label="drifting"></talos-compass>
  <talos-compass heading="140" target="90" warn="10" crit="25" label="off target"></talos-compass>
</Demo>

```html
<talos-compass heading="105" target="90" warn="10" crit="25" label="drifting"></talos-compass>
```

With `target` set, `warn` / `crit` are **off-target tolerances** in degrees of
shortest-angle error: the needle turns amber, then red, as the heading drifts
off the target bearing. Without a target the needle is always nominal; a bearing
alone has no wrong value.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `heading` | `0` | current bearing in degrees; wraps at 360 |
| `target` | — | desired bearing, drawn as a marker |
| `warn` | — | off-target error (degrees) at which the band is warning |
| `crit` | — | off-target error (degrees) at which the band is critical |
| `label` | — | caption under the readout |
| `size` | `160` | px square |

All attributes are reactive.
